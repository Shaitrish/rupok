# Code of Conduct

Rupok adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**
as its code of conduct. The full text at that link is the binding version;
this page is a summary.

## In short

Rupok is built by and for teachers, students and developers from many
countries, backgrounds and levels of experience. We want participation in
this project to be harassment-free for everyone.

**Do:**

- be kind, patient and welcoming, especially to newcomers and to teachers who
  are not developers;
- respect different viewpoints and experiences;
- give and accept constructive feedback gracefully;
- take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them;
- focus on what is best for the community and for learners.

**Don't:**

- use sexualised language or imagery, or make unwelcome advances;
- troll, insult, make derogatory comments, or attack people personally or
  politically;
- harass anyone, publicly or privately;
- publish others' private information without their explicit permission;
- do anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting or
  a classroom.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions,
chat, events) and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **conduct@shaitrish.com** *(placeholder
address until the project's contact is set up)*. All reports are reviewed
promptly and fairly, and kept confidential. Maintainers will not retaliate
against anyone who reports in good faith.

Maintainers enforce this code following the Contributor Covenant 2.1
[Enforcement Guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
correction, warning, temporary ban or permanent ban, depending on the
impact.
