Open the board — it's free View the source

No account, no install. Runs in Chrome or Edge on any smartboard, laptop or tablet.

Everything a class needs. Nothing in the way.

Built for 86-inch panels and chalk-dusted fingers, and just as good on a laptop.

A pen that keeps up

A GPU ink engine draws in under a millisecond, even after a two-hour class with thousands of strokes. Pressure, palm rejection, highlighter, shapes.

Live Smart Objects

Diagrams, simulations, quizzes and games run right on the slide. Write on top of them, and step through them with your clicker.

Your slides in one drop

Drop a PDF and every page becomes a slide you can write on. Images, HTML and lessons drop in the same way.

Every class, replayable

Every stroke is timestamped. Replay any page, stroke by stroke, for students who missed it.

Works offline

Lessons are single .rpk files. Fonts and libraries are built in, so a classroom without Wi-Fi still works. Install it as an app.

Yours to shape

Light or dark, seven accents, four icon styles, toolbar on any side, sizes for big boards. Chalkboard and paper backgrounds too.

Your AI, your subscription

Ask for a lesson.
Teach it a minute later.

Rupok speaks MCP, the open standard for AI tools. Connect the agent you already use — Claude, Codex, OpenCode — and ask for “a binary counter for Grade 7” or “a quiz on networking”. It builds the slides and Smart Objects straight into your lesson.

  • Uses your own AI subscription. Rupok never sees your login.
  • Every object is validated and sandboxed before it reaches the board.
  • Coming with Rupok Cloud: a one-click connector inside Claude.
# connect your agent once
$ claude mcp add rupok -- rupok mcp

# then just ask
› Make tomorrow’s ICT class on binary numbers:
  5 slides, a live counter, and a 4-question quiz.
