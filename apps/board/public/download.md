The free whiteboard for teachers, as a desktop app for Windows, Mac and Linux. Or open it in Chrome, Edge or Safari on any device: same board, no install.

## Every platform

## Install as an app

On a phone, tablet or Chromebook, the web app works like an installed one: its own window, offline, on the home screen.

### Android, Chromebook, Windows

Open [rupok.app/board](https://rupok.app/board/) in Chrome, Edge or Safari, open the browser menu and choose **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).

### iPhone and iPad

Open [rupok.app/board](https://rupok.app/board/) in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.

## A note on the warnings

The installers aren’t code-signed yet, so your computer may ask once before it opens Rupok. That’s expected; signing is on the roadmap.

- **Windows:** if SmartScreen says “Windows protected your PC”, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Mac:** right-click Rupok in Applications, choose **Open**, then **Open** again. Or open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and choose **Open Anyway**.

## System requirements

- **Windows**: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
- **macOS**: macOS 11 Big Sur or later, Apple silicon or Intel
- **Linux**: 64-bit; .deb for Debian and Ubuntu, AppImage for other distributions
- **Browser**: A recent Chrome, Edge or Safari (any OS), with WebGL2 or WebGPU
- **Hardware**: Any smartboard, interactive panel, laptop or tablet; a pen is optional
- **Account**: None
