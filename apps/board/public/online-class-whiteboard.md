# A whiteboard for online classes that students can replay

> Teach online classes on Zoom, Google Meet or Teams with a free whiteboard: write on slides with a pen tablet, add live AI activities, then send students a replay they open in the browser.

Canonical: https://rupok.app/online-class-whiteboard/ · Language: en · Updated: 2026-11-23

Share Rupok in Zoom, Google Meet or Teams and teach with a pen tablet, a touchscreen or a mouse. After class, send one small file to the class group: students replay every stroke in their browser, with the live activities still working.

## Set up your online class in three minutes

1. **Open the board.** Go to [rupok.app/board](https://rupok.app/board/) in Chrome, Edge or Safari and drop in your PDF slides.
2. **Share the window.** In Zoom, Meet or Teams, share the Rupok tab or window. Teach mode keeps the slide fitted with minimal chrome.
3. **Teach with a pen.** A pen tablet (Wacom, XP-Pen, Huion), a pen-enabled laptop or an Android tablet with a stylus gives you pressure-sensitive ink. A touchscreen or a mouse works too.
4. **Send the replay.** Save the lesson as a `.rpk` file and post it in the class WhatsApp group, Google Classroom or email.

## A replay instead of a recording

A screen recording is a heavy video your students have to download. Rupok saves your strokes, not pixels, so the lesson file is usually much smaller than a video of the same class.

Students open it at [rupok.app/open](https://rupok.app/open/), press play and watch the class unfold at 1–8× speed. They can pause, zoom in on a diagram, and use the simulations themselves.

- **Small files**: Strokes, not video, so it works on mobile data.
- **Still interactive**: Simulations and quizzes keep working in the replay.
- **No account**: Students just open the file in their browser.

## Tips for teaching online with a digital whiteboard

- **Use a pen.** Even a cheap pen tablet makes handwriting and diagrams far clearer than a mouse.
- **Share one window, not your whole screen,** so notifications stay private.
- **Use the laser pointer** to show where you are without leaving marks.
- **Prepare live objects in advance:** ask your AI for the simulation the night before, so class time is for teaching.
- **Keep slides simple:** big text survives video compression on slow connections.

## Questions people ask

### What is the best whiteboard for online teaching?

One you can write on comfortably with a pen, that shares cleanly over video, and that leaves students something to review. Rupok does all three for free, and adds live AI-made activities and a replay file.

### Does Rupok work with Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams?

Yes. Share the Rupok tab or window like any other app. Nothing needs to be installed in the meeting.

### Do I need a pen tablet?

No, but it helps a lot. Rupok supports pen pressure from pen tablets and pen-enabled screens in Chrome, Edge or Safari; touch and mouse work too.

### How do students watch the class later?

Send them the `.rpk` lesson file. They open it at rupok.app/open on a phone or computer and press play. No account is needed.

### Is Rupok a digital whiteboard or an interactive whiteboard?

Both: a digital whiteboard you can use on a laptop for online classes, and interactive whiteboard software for a smartboard in a classroom.
