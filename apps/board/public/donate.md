# Help keep Rupok free for every teacher

> Rupok is free and open source, with no ads or tracking. Donate once by bKash or US bank transfer to pay for signing certificates, servers, test devices and development time, or help by starring, sharing and translating.

Canonical: https://rupok.app/donate/ · Language: en · Updated: 2026-11-23

Rupok is free and open source, and it stays free whether or not you donate. Donations pay for what a free app still costs: signing certificates, servers, test devices and the time to build it.

## Why support Rupok

- **Free for every teacher:** every feature, on every device, with no account.
- **Open source:** the code is on GitHub under the AGPL.
- **Made in Bangladesh:** built by Shaitrish in Dhaka, with Bangla in mind from day one.
- **No ads, no tracking:** no analytics, no tracking cookies, nothing sold.

## What your donation pays for

Nothing goes to ads or marketing. This is where the money goes.

- **Signing certificates:** Apple's developer membership ($99 a year) and a Windows code-signing certificate, so installs stop showing warnings.
- **Servers:** rupok.app, downloads, and the free Rupok Cloud plan.
- **Devices for testing:** interactive panels, pen tablets, a Mac and Android tablets.
- **Development time:** fixes, new features, the Bangla interface and support.

## Questions about donating

### Is Rupok still free if I don't donate?

Yes, completely. Every feature works the same for everyone. Donations don't unlock anything; they help us keep building.

### What's the difference between a donation and Rupok Cloud?

A donation is a gift to the project. Rupok Cloud is an optional hosted service (sync, sharing, live classes) that will have a free plan for every teacher and paid plans for more storage. The app itself is free either way.

### Can I pay by card, PayPal or GitHub Sponsors?

Not yet. Today you can donate by bKash or by US bank transfer (ACH, including from Wise). If neither works for you, email [jahangir@shaitrish.com](mailto:jahangir@shaitrish.com) and we'll find a way.

### Can I donate every month?

There's no subscription. If you'd like to give regularly, set up a repeat transfer in your bank or bKash app, with Rupok as the reference.

### Will my name appear on this page?

Only if you ask. We never list donors by default. Email us after donating if you'd like your name, or your school's, on the thank-you list.

### Does donating share my data or track me?

No. This page has no analytics or payment scripts. We only see what your bank or bKash shows us for the transfer (usually a name or a number), and we use it only to say thank you.

### Where do the numbers at the top come from?

They are read live from GitHub's public API: the total downloads of every desktop release, the repository's stars, and the number of releases. If GitHub can't be reached, the page links to the numbers instead of guessing.

### Can my school or company sponsor Rupok?

Yes, and thank you. Email [jahangir@shaitrish.com](mailto:jahangir@shaitrish.com) and we'll talk about what helps most, such as test devices or a year of signing certificates.
