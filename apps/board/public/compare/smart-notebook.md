# A free SMART Notebook alternative for any board

> A free, open-source alternative to SMART Notebook that works on any brand of smartboard. Compare price, platforms, AI, replay and languages, with sources. Checked November 2026.

Canonical: https://rupok.app/compare/smart-notebook/ · Language: en · Updated: 2026-11-23

SMART Notebook is the most established smartboard software, with a free basic version and paid Plus features. Rupok is free with every feature, open source, and works the same on any brand of panel. Here’s where each one wins.

## Where SMART Notebook is stronger

Being honest about this saves you time.

- **Decades of classroom use**, and a huge base of `.notebook` lessons shared between teachers.
- **Game-based activities and assessments on student devices** through Lumio, with LMS integrations (Canvas, Schoology, D2L) on the Spark plan[^6].
- **AI lesson and quiz generation is shipping** in Lumio[^7].
- **Deep SMART hardware support:** pens, ink and touch tuned for SMART Boards.
- **Imports ActivInspire flipcharts**, with bulk conversion[^4].

## Where Rupok is stronger

- **Every feature is free, on any brand.** SMART’s free version limits touch on non-SMART displays[^2]; Rupok works the same on a SMART, Promethean, BenQ, ViewSonic or Prestigio panel.
- **Runs on Linux and ChromeOS too**, in Chrome or Edge. Nothing to install.
- **AI that builds real simulations,** not only quizzes: projectile motion, 3D molecules, circuits. It works with the AI you already use, including Claude through MCP.
- **Class replay students can open.** They replay every stroke from a small `.rpk` file, in the browser, with no account.
- **Bangla text in lessons**, and an open-source codebase and file format.

## Which one should you pick?

**Pick SMART Notebook** if your school runs SMART Boards, uses Lumio’s student-device games or LMS integrations, or has years of `.notebook` lessons.

**Pick Rupok** if your boards are mixed brands, you want every feature free, or you want AI-made simulations and class replays.

## Switching from SMART Notebook

1. **Save your Notebook lesson as a PDF.** Export or print it to PDF from SMART Notebook.
2. **Open the board.** Go to [rupok.app/board](https://rupok.app/board/) in Chrome or Edge, on the panel's PC or your laptop. No install, no account.
3. **Drop the PDF in.** Every page becomes a slide you can write on.
4. **Add the live parts.** Ask your AI for a simulation or quiz and drop it on a slide as a Smart Object ([how](https://rupok.app/ai-whiteboard/)).
5. **Save and share.** Save the lesson as a `.rpk` file and send it to students. They replay it at [rupok.app/open](https://rupok.app/open/).

## Rupok and SMART Notebook: questions

### Is SMART Notebook free?

The basic desktop version is free. Notebook Plus with Lumio Standard costs US$59 a year[^1], and full touch on non-SMART displays needs Plus[^2]. Rupok is free with every feature.

### What is the best free alternative to SMART Notebook?

For a free desktop app with a long history, OpenBoard. For a free browser app with AI-made interactive lessons and class replay, Rupok. Both are open source.

### Can Rupok open .notebook files?

Not yet. Save the lesson as a PDF from SMART Notebook and drop it into Rupok to write on every page.

### Does Rupok work on a SMART Board?

Yes. Rupok runs in Chrome or Edge on the computer connected to the board, and works with the board’s touch and pen like any browser app.
