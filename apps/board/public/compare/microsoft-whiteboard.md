> Microsoft retired the standalone Whiteboard apps in October 2026 and personal accounts can no longer edit boards. Rupok is a free, no-account whiteboard for teachers. Compare, with sources.

Canonical: https://rupok.app/compare/microsoft-whiteboard/ · Language: en · Updated: 2026-11-23

In 2026 Microsoft retired the standalone Whiteboard apps, and personal Microsoft accounts lost the ability to create or edit boards. Whiteboard lives on for work and school accounts, on the web and in Teams. If that leaves you out, here’s how Rupok compares.

## Where Microsoft Whiteboard is stronger

Being honest about this saves you time.

- **Real-time collaboration:** many people drawing on one board at once, with cursors, live in Teams meetings[^5]. Rupok’s live class is still coming.
- **Teams and OneDrive integration**, with school identity, compliance and storage managed by IT.
- **Copilot** to suggest, sort and summarize ideas, if your school pays for Copilot[^3].

## Where Rupok is stronger

- **No account at all.** Teachers and students just open it; personal Microsoft accounts can no longer edit Whiteboard boards[^2].
- **Made for presenting lessons:** write on PDF slides, Teach mode for the board, clicker support.
- **AI Smart Objects:** live simulations and quizzes on your slide.
- **Class replay** students open from a `.rpk` file.
- **PDF in and out:** import a PDF to teach from, and export your lesson as PDF slides or note-taking handouts.
- **Open source, with Bangla text in lessons.**

## Which one should you pick?

**Pick Microsoft Whiteboard** if your school is on Microsoft 365 and you need live co-editing in Teams meetings.

**Pick Rupok** if you teach from slides on a smartboard, don’t have a work or school Microsoft account, or want AI-made interactive lessons and replays.

## Moving your boards to Rupok

1. **Export your board.** Whiteboard exports boards as images[^4]; drop the images into Rupok, or print the board to PDF.
2. **Open the board.** Go to [rupok.app/board](https://rupok.app/board/) in Chrome, Edge or Safari, on the panel's PC or your laptop. No install, no account. Prefer an app? [Download it](https://rupok.app/download/) for Windows, Mac or Linux.
3. **Drop the PDF in.** Every page becomes a slide you can write on.
4. **Add the live parts.** Ask your AI for a simulation or quiz and drop it on a slide as a Smart Object ([how](https://rupok.app/ai-whiteboard/)).
5. **Save and share.** Save the lesson as a `.rpk` file and send it to students. They replay it at [rupok.app/open](https://rupok.app/open/).

## Rupok and Microsoft Whiteboard: questions

### Is Microsoft Whiteboard being discontinued?

The standalone apps for Windows, iOS and Android retired on 16 October 2026. For work and school accounts, Whiteboard continues on the web and in Teams[^1].

### Can I still use Microsoft Whiteboard with a personal account?

Since 25 September 2026, personal Microsoft accounts can no longer create or edit whiteboards[^2].

### Is Rupok a good free replacement?

For teaching from slides on a board or in an online class, yes: it’s free, needs no account, and adds AI Smart Objects and replay. For live co-editing with a group, it isn’t there yet; that’s coming with Rupok Cloud.
