# Rupok vs OneNote for teaching on a board

> OneNote is a great notebook; Rupok is built for teaching from a smartboard. Compare price, ink replay, AI, Class Notebook, platforms and Bangla. Facts checked November 2026.

Canonical: https://rupok.app/compare/onenote/ · Language: en · Updated: 2026-11-23

Many teachers write on OneNote at the front of the class. OneNote is a superb notebook for every device. Rupok is built for presenting: slides, a Teach mode for the board, live AI-made objects, and a replay students can open anywhere.

## Where OneNote is stronger

Being honest about this saves you time.

- **Notebooks on every device**, with offline work and sync[^1][^9].
- **Class Notebook:** private student sections, a read-only content library, a collaboration space, page distribution and feedback, with rosters from Teams or an LMS[^3].
- **Co-editing** and a huge Microsoft 365 ecosystem; Office 365 A1 is free for eligible schools[^7].
- **Math Assistant and handwriting search** (Math Assistant needs a qualifying subscription). Rupok reads handwritten maths offline, and Solve this writes worked solutions with your own AI.

## Where Rupok is stronger

- **Built for presenting:** slide pages, a Teach mode that keeps the slide fitted to the board, and clicker support.
- **Live Smart Objects:** simulations and quizzes made by your AI, running on the slide.
- **Replay anywhere:** students replay every stroke in any browser. OneNote’s Ink Replay is Windows-only[^2].
- **No account** for teachers or students.
- **Fast GPU ink** that stays smooth through a long class.
- **Open source,** with Bangla text in lessons.

## Which one should you pick?

**Pick OneNote** for class notebooks, homework distribution and notes across every device in a Microsoft 365 school.

**Pick Rupok** for teaching at the board: writing on slides, live AI-made objects, and replays for students.

Plenty of teachers use both: OneNote for the notebook, Rupok for the lesson.

## Moving a OneNote lesson to Rupok

1. **Export the pages as PDF.** OneNote exports pages, sections or notebooks to PDF on Windows, and single pages on the web and Mac[^8].
2. **Open the board.** Go to [rupok.app/board](https://rupok.app/board/) in Chrome, Edge or Safari, on the panel's PC or your laptop. No install, no account. Prefer an app? [Download it](https://rupok.app/download/) for Windows, Mac or Linux.
3. **Drop the PDF in.** Every page becomes a slide you can write on.
4. **Add the live parts.** Ask your AI for a simulation or quiz and drop it on a slide as a Smart Object ([how](https://rupok.app/ai-whiteboard/)).
5. **Save and share.** Save the lesson as a `.rpk` file and send it to students. They replay it at [rupok.app/open](https://rupok.app/open/).

## Rupok and OneNote: questions

### Is OneNote free for teachers?

OneNote is free to download and use. Some features, such as Ink Replay and Math Assistant, and Copilot need a Microsoft 365 or Copilot subscription[^1][^5]. Eligible schools can get Office 365 A1 for free[^7].

### Does OneNote replay handwriting?

Yes, Ink Replay redraws strokes in order, but only on Windows[^2]. Rupok’s replay works in any browser, including students’ phones.

### Should I switch from OneNote to Rupok?

Not necessarily. Keep OneNote for notebooks and use Rupok for presenting at the board, where slides, live objects and replay matter.
