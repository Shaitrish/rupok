# A free ActivInspire alternative for any panel

> A free, open-source alternative to Promethean ActivInspire for any interactive panel. Compare editions, price, platforms, AI and recording. Facts checked November 2026.

Canonical: https://rupok.app/compare/activinspire/ · Language: en · Updated: 2026-11-23

ActivInspire is Promethean’s long-running flipchart software, with a free Personal Edition and a paid Professional Edition. Rupok is free with every feature, runs in the browser, and adds AI-made lessons and class replay.

## Where ActivInspire is stronger

Being honest about this saves you time.

- **Years of flipcharts.** Many schools have large libraries of `.flipchart` lessons and Promethean resource packs.
- **Deep authoring tools** in Professional: actions, the question manager and self-paced learning[^1].
- **Learner-response hardware** (ActivVote, ActivExpression) and Express Poll[^1].
- **Imports SMART Notebook files**[^1] and exports IWB files[^6].

## Where Rupok is stronger

- **Every feature is free.** Replay, PDF import and PDF export (including handouts) aren’t locked behind a paid edition.
- **Runs in the browser**, on Windows, macOS, Linux and ChromeOS, with nothing to install, or as a desktop app for Windows, Mac and Linux.
- **AI Smart Objects:** live simulations and quizzes from the AI you already use.
- **Replay for every student:** a small `.rpk` file they open in any browser.
- **Bangla text in lessons** and an open file format, so lessons aren’t locked to one vendor.
- **Actively developed** in the open, with a public roadmap.

## Which one should you pick?

**Pick ActivInspire** if your school has a big flipchart library, uses Promethean learner-response devices, or needs Professional’s authoring tools.

**Pick Rupok** if you want every feature free on any panel, AI-made interactive lessons, and replays students can open at home.

## Switching from ActivInspire

1. **Export from ActivInspire.** Professional exports flipcharts to PDF[^1]; with Personal Edition, print to PDF.
2. **Open the board.** Go to [rupok.app/board](https://rupok.app/board/) in Chrome, Edge or Safari, on the panel's PC or your laptop. No install, no account. Prefer an app? [Download it](https://rupok.app/download/) for Windows, Mac or Linux.
3. **Drop the PDF in.** Every page becomes a slide you can write on.
4. **Add the live parts.** Ask your AI for a simulation or quiz and drop it on a slide as a Smart Object ([how](https://rupok.app/ai-whiteboard/)).
5. **Save and share.** Save the lesson as a `.rpk` file and send it to students. They replay it at [rupok.app/open](https://rupok.app/open/).

## Rupok and ActivInspire: questions

### Is ActivInspire being discontinued?

We found no announced end date as of November 2026. Promethean’s newest classroom software is ActivSuite[^4]. It retired ClassFlow at the end of 2023, saying that wouldn’t affect ActivInspire[^5].

### Is ActivInspire free?

The Personal Edition is a free download. The Professional Edition comes with some ActivPanels or costs US$1,249 per device on Promethean’s January 2026 US price list[^1][^2].

### Can Rupok open .flipchart files?

No. Export or print the flipchart to PDF and drop it into Rupok. (SMART Notebook can import flipcharts, if you need the objects kept.)

### Does Rupok work on a Promethean ActivPanel?

Yes, from Chrome, Edge or Safari on the panel’s computer. Rupok works with any panel’s touch and pen like any browser app.
