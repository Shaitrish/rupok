# An Explain Everything alternative with free, unlimited replay

> Compare Rupok with Explain Everything: free plan limits, pricing, platforms, recording vs stroke replay, AI and languages. Facts checked November 2026, with sources.

Canonical: https://rupok.app/compare/explain-everything/ · Language: en · Updated: 2026-11-23

Explain Everything, now part of Promethean, records polished video lessons on tablets, with a limited free plan. Rupok replays every stroke of your class from a small file, free and without limits, and runs on your classroom board.

## Where Explain Everything is stronger

Being honest about this saves you time.

- **Polished video lessons:** record with your voice, edit, add captions and export MP4[^2]. Rupok replays strokes; replay with audio is coming.
- **Native tablet apps** for iPad, iPhone and Android that work offline[^1][^4].
- **Real-time collaboration** on paid plans[^1].
- **Many export formats:** video, PDF, images and GIFs[^2].
- **On-device AI** for activities and lesson plans on recent Apple devices[^2][^5].

## Where Rupok is stronger

- **No limits on the free plan:** unlimited lessons, pages and replay length.
- **Replay, not video:** students get every stroke in a small file. They can pause, zoom and still use the live Smart Objects, which a video can’t do.
- **AI from any assistant**, not tied to an Apple device or a paid plan: Claude, ChatGPT, Codex or OpenCode.
- **Built for the classroom board:** fast GPU ink on smartboards and panels in Chrome or Edge.
- **Bangla text in lessons**, and open source.

## Which one should you pick?

**Pick Explain Everything** if you make narrated video lessons on an iPad, or need collaboration on tablets.

**Pick Rupok** if you teach live on a board or in an online class and want free, unlimited replays and AI-made interactive lessons.

## Switching from Explain Everything

1. **Export your project as PDF.** Explain Everything exports PDF[^2].
2. **Open the board.** Go to [rupok.app/board](https://rupok.app/board/) in Chrome or Edge, on the panel's PC or your laptop. No install, no account.
3. **Drop the PDF in.** Every page becomes a slide you can write on.
4. **Add the live parts.** Ask your AI for a simulation or quiz and drop it on a slide as a Smart Object ([how](https://rupok.app/ai-whiteboard/)).
5. **Save and share.** Save the lesson as a `.rpk` file and send it to students. They replay it at [rupok.app/open](https://rupok.app/open/).

## Rupok and Explain Everything: questions

### Is Explain Everything free?

There’s a free plan with 3 projects, 1 slide per project and 3-minute videos. Paid plans start at US$69 a year for education (Advanced) or US$7.49 a month billed yearly (Pro)[^1].

### Who owns Explain Everything?

Promethean acquired Explain Everything in November 2022[^3].

### How is Rupok’s replay different from a video?

Rupok saves each pen stroke with its timing, so a replay redraws the lesson. The file stays small, students can zoom in, and live Smart Objects keep working. It has no sound yet; replay with audio is coming.
