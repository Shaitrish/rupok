# Plan it, teach it, replay it

> Rupok is a free whiteboard and note-taking app for teachers: prepare lessons the night before, teach on the smartboard, and share a replay with students. Open source, with Bangla text.

Canonical: https://rupok.app/for-teachers/ · Language: en · Updated: 2026-11-23

Rupok follows a teacher’s day. Prepare slides and live activities at home, teach on the classroom board, and send students the class, stroke by stroke, so nothing you write is wiped away.

## A teaching day with Rupok

### The night before: Prepare

Drop in your PDF slides, add text and images, and ask your AI for the interactive parts: a circuit, a titration, a quiz. Prepare mode has the full toolbar and page list.

### In class: Teach

Teach mode keeps the slide fitted to the board and the chrome out of the way. Write with the pen, use the live objects with touch, press the clicker to move on.

### After class: Share

Save the lesson as one `.rpk` file and post it in the class group. Students open it at [rupok.app/open](https://rupok.app/open/) in Student mode: read-only, with replay.

### Next year: Reuse

Open last year’s lesson, replay how you explained it, and build on it. Your lessons are files you own, in an open format.

## Notes that don’t get wiped

On a chalkboard, the best explanation of the year is gone by the next period. In Rupok every page is kept, and every stroke is stored with its timing, so a page can be replayed exactly as you wrote it.

- **Every page kept**: Add as many pages as the class needs; reorder and duplicate them.
- **Replay at 1–8×**: Students who missed class watch it unfold in order.
- **Download PDF**: Print the pages with your ink on them for revision.

## Questions people ask

### Is Rupok a note-taking app for teachers?

It’s a whiteboard first, made for presenting at the board, but it keeps everything: pages, ink, live objects and the timing of every stroke. Many teachers use it as their teaching notebook.

### Can students edit my lesson?

No. Students open lessons in Student mode, which is read-only. They can replay it and use the live objects.

### Where are my lessons saved?

On your device: Rupok autosaves in your browser, and you save `.rpk` files wherever you like. Cloud sync is coming with Rupok Cloud.

### Does it work on a laptop without a smartboard?

Yes. Use a mouse, touchscreen or pen tablet, and present through a projector or in an online class.
