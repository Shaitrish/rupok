- We only use the other product’s own pages (pricing, release notes, help centre, app store listing), and say when a source is a mirror or a reseller copy.
- Every claim has a date. Software changes; if we’re out of date, [tell us on GitHub](https://github.com/Shaitrish/rupok/issues) and we’ll fix it.

- No logos, no made-up numbers.

## Questions people ask

### What is the best smartboard software?

It depends on your boards and what you teach. SMART Notebook and ActivInspire suit schools invested in SMART or Promethean hardware. OpenBoard is a solid free desktop app. Rupok is free, open source and works on any board, with AI-made interactive lessons and class replay.

### What is the best free smartboard software?

The free options are Rupok and OpenBoard (both open source), plus the free basic editions of SMART Notebook and ActivInspire. Only Rupok and OpenBoard are free with every feature on any brand of board.

### Is there open-source smartboard software?

Yes: Rupok (AGPL-3.0, runs in the browser) and OpenBoard (GPL-3.0, desktop app).

### Which whiteboard software supports Bangla?

Rupok supports Bangla text and fonts in lessons today, and its Bangla interface is coming. Note3, SMART Notebook, OpenBoard, ActivInspire and Explain Everything don’t list Bangla among their interface languages. Microsoft 365 apps such as OneNote offer Bangla proofing tools and partial Bangla (India) localization.
