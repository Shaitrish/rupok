## Questions people ask

### Is Rupok really free?

Yes. The app is open source (AGPL-3.0) and every board feature is free forever, on every device. You only pay for Rupok Cloud extras: more storage, unlimited student links, full history, hosted AI and, soon, live classes.

### What stays free forever?

The whole app, offline, with plain `.rpk` files; every board feature, including future ones; using your own Claude, Codex or OpenCode; the 500 MB cloud plan; student links students open with no account; exporting and deleting your data.

### What happens if I stop paying?

Nothing is deleted. You keep every lesson, can open, download and share them, and can delete things to get back under the free limits. Only new uploads stop while you're over 500 MB.

### How do I pay from Bangladesh?

In taka, with bKash, Nagad, Rocket, a card or internet banking, through SSLCommerz. Buy 1, 6 or 12 months at a time; nothing renews automatically, and the app reminds you before your time runs out.

### How do I pay from other countries?

In US dollars by card, PayPal, Apple Pay or Google Pay, through Paddle. Paddle is our reseller: it handles tax and invoices. Change plan, update your card or cancel any time from Settings → Account → Manage subscription.

### What are Rupok AI credits?

Credits run Rupok's hosted AI (Solve this, Design with AI, the chat) for teachers who don't have their own AI subscription. A chat reply or a quick solution uses about 1 credit, a full step-by-step solution about 4, and designing a Smart Object about 10. Using your own Claude Code, Codex or OpenCode is always free.

### Do you offer refunds?

We plan a 14-day, no-questions refund on your first purchase. The exact policy will be in our terms before payments open.

### Can my school pay by invoice?

Yes. School plans and classroom board licences are invoiced once a year and paid by bank transfer, with a VAT receipt in Bangladesh. [Talk to us](https://shaitrish.com) for a quote or a free pilot.

### Do students pay?

Never. Students open lessons from a link with no account, and a student account is free.

### Is there a free trial?

Yes: 14 days of Pro, no card needed, once per account, when paid plans open.
